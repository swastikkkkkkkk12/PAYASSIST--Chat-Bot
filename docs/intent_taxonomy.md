# Intent Taxonomy

## PAYMENT

### 1. PAYMENT_FAILED
Customer's payment failed.

Example:
"My payment failed."

### 2. MONEY_DEDUCTED_FAILED
Payment failed but money was deducted.

Example:
"₹2000 was deducted but payment failed."

### 3. PAYMENT_PENDING
Payment is still pending.

Example:
"My UPI payment is pending."

### 4. PAYMENT_REVERSED
Payment was reversed.

Example:
"Why was my payment reversed?"

### 5. DUPLICATE_TRANSACTION
Customer believes they were charged twice.

Example:
"I was charged twice for the same payment."

### 6. PAYMENT_NOT_RECEIVED
Payment shows successful but recipient/merchant has not received it.

Example:
"The payment says successful but the merchant didn't get it."


## REFUND

### 7. REFUND_PENDING
Refund has been initiated but is still pending.

Example:
"My refund is pending."

### 8. REFUND_NOT_RECEIVED
Customer has not received an expected refund.

Example:
"I haven't received my refund."

### 9. REFUND_FAILED
Refund attempt failed.

Example:
"My refund failed."


## UPI

### 10. UPI_LIMIT
Customer asks about UPI limits.

Example:
"What is my UPI transaction limit?"

### 11. UPI_PIN
Customer asks about UPI PIN.

Example:
"I forgot my UPI PIN."


## ACCOUNT

### 12. KYC_STATUS
Customer asks about KYC.

Example:
"Why is my KYC still pending?"

### 13. ACCOUNT_ACCESS
Customer has difficulty accessing the account.

Example:
"I can't log into my account."


## SECURITY

### 14. UNAUTHORIZED_TRANSACTION
Customer doesn't recognize a transaction.

Example:
"I don't recognize this payment."

### 15. SUSPICIOUS_ACTIVITY
Customer reports suspicious activity.

Example:
"I think someone is trying to access my account."


## SUPPORT

### 16. COMPLAINT_STATUS
Customer wants to know the status of an existing complaint.

Example:
"What is the status of my complaint?"

### 17. CREATE_COMPLAINT
Customer wants to create a complaint.

Example:
"I want to raise a complaint."

### 18. HUMAN_ESCALATION
Customer wants to talk to a human.

Example:
"Connect me with a support agent."
