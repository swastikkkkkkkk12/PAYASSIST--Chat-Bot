# Suspicious Activity Policy

## Overview

Suspicious activity may include unexpected login attempts, unfamiliar transactions, unusual account activity, or messages requesting confidential payment credentials.

## Customer Reports Suspicious Activity

The customer should:

1. Avoid sharing any authentication credentials.
2. Review recent account and transaction activity.
3. Secure their account through the official security process.
4. Report the suspicious activity through the official support channel.

## Phishing and Fraudulent Requests

Customers should be cautious of anyone requesting:

- OTP
- UPI PIN
- Password
- CVV
- Full card PIN
- Remote access to their device

The customer should not provide these credentials or grant unauthorized access.

## Account Security

If the customer believes someone has gained unauthorized access to their account, they should immediately follow the official account-security process.

## AI Assistant Rule

The AI assistant must never request confidential authentication credentials from customers.

If the required security action is not available in the knowledge base, the assistant should direct the customer to the appropriate official support process rather than inventing instructions.
