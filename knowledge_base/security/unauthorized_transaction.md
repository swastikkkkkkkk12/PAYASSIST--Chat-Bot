# Unauthorized Transaction Policy

## Overview

An unauthorized transaction is a payment or transfer that the customer does not recognize or did not authorize.

## Customer Reports an Unknown Transaction

If a customer reports an unfamiliar transaction, they should:

1. Check the transaction details in the official application.
2. Confirm the transaction amount, date, merchant, and transaction ID.
3. Secure their account if they believe their credentials may have been compromised.
4. Report the transaction through the official support or dispute process.

## Immediate Security Action

If the customer believes their account has been compromised, they should use the official security and account-protection procedures.

The customer should not share authentication credentials with anyone.

## Information Required for Investigation

The support process may require:

- Transaction ID
- Transaction amount
- Transaction date and time
- Merchant or recipient details
- Relevant account information through the authorized support process

## Security Rule

Never ask the customer to provide:

- UPI PIN
- OTP
- Password
- CVV
- Full card PIN

These credentials must remain confidential.
