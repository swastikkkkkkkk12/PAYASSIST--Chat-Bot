# KYC Frequently Asked Questions

## Why is my KYC still pending?

KYC may remain pending while submitted information or documents are being reviewed.

The customer should check the KYC section of the application for any pending actions or additional requirements.

## How can I check my KYC status?

The customer should open the KYC section of the official payment application and review the current verification status.

## What should I do if my KYC was rejected?

The customer should review the reason provided for rejection and follow the official instructions for correcting the information or resubmitting the required documents.

## Can customer support manually verify my KYC?

The AI assistant should not claim that it can manually verify a customer's identity.

KYC verification should be performed through the authorized verification process.

## My KYC information is incorrect

The customer should use the official account or KYC update process to correct the information.

If the option is unavailable, the customer should contact the appropriate support channel.

## Security Rule

Never ask the customer to provide:

- OTP
- UPI PIN
- Password
- CVV
- Full card PIN
