# KYC Verification Process

## What is KYC?

KYC stands for Know Your Customer.

It is an identity verification process used to verify customer information.

## General KYC Process

A customer may be required to:

1. Open the KYC section in the official application.
2. Provide the requested personal information.
3. Submit the required identity documents.
4. Complete any required verification steps.
5. Wait for the verification process to be completed.

## Document Requirements

The exact documents required for KYC may vary depending on the customer's circumstances and applicable requirements.

The customer should follow the document requirements displayed in the official application.

## Incorrect Information

If submitted information is incorrect, the customer should follow the official process to update or correct the information.

## Security

Customers should submit identity documents only through official and trusted channels.

The AI assistant must never request unnecessary sensitive credentials such as:

- UPI PIN
- OTP
- Password
- CVV
- Full card PIN
