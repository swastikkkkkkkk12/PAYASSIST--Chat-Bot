# KYC Status Policy

## Overview

KYC status indicates the current state of the customer's identity verification process.

Possible statuses may include:

- Not Started
- Pending
- Under Review
- Verified
- Rejected
- Expired

## KYC Pending

If KYC is pending, the verification process may still be under review.

The customer should:

1. Check the KYC status in the application.
2. Verify that the submitted information is correct.
3. Check whether any additional documents or actions are requested.
4. Wait for the verification process to complete.

## KYC Rejected

If KYC is rejected, the customer should check the reason provided in the application and follow the available instructions for correcting the issue or resubmitting the required information.

## KYC Verified

If KYC is verified, the customer's identity verification process has been successfully completed.

## Important

The AI assistant should not claim that a customer's KYC is verified, rejected, or pending unless the actual account status is available through an authorized system.
