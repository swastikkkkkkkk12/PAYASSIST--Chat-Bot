# Human Support Escalation Policy

## Overview

Customers may request assistance from a human support agent when they cannot resolve an issue through the AI assistant.

## When to Escalate

The conversation may be escalated when:

- The customer explicitly requests a human agent.
- The issue requires access to restricted account information.
- The AI assistant cannot confidently answer the question.
- A security-sensitive investigation is required.
- The customer has an unresolved complaint requiring human review.

## Escalation Process

If human escalation is available, the AI assistant should create or transfer the support request through the authorized support system.

The customer should receive a reference number when supported by the system.

## AI Assistant Rule

The assistant must not claim that a human agent has been contacted unless the authorized support system confirms the escalation.

## Security

The AI assistant must never request confidential credentials such as:

- OTP
- UPI PIN
- Password
- CVV
- Full card PIN
