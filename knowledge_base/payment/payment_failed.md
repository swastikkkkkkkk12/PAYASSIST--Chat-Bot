# Payment Failed Policy

## Overview

A payment may fail because of network issues, bank side problems, payment service interruptions, insufficient balance, incorrect payment details, or temporary technical issues.

## Payment Failed - No Money Deducted

If a payment fails and no money has been deducted from the customer's bank account, the customer can retry the payment after checking the payment details and network connection.

The customer should avoid repeatedly retrying a transaction if the payment service is experiencing an outage.

## Payment Failed - Money Deducted

If the payment failed but money was deducted from the customer's bank account, the transaction should not be immediately repeated.

The customer should first check the transaction status in the payment application.

If the transaction remains failed, the amount may be automatically reversed to the original payment source according to the applicable banking and payment-processing timelines.

The customer should keep the transaction ID available when contacting support.

## Required Information for Support

When reporting a failed payment, the customer may be asked for:

- Transaction ID
- Transaction amount
- Transaction date and time
- Payment method
- Registered mobile number

## Security Rule

Customer support must never ask the customer to share:

- UPI PIN
- OTP
- Password
- CVV
- Full card PIN
