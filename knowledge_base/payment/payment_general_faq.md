# Payment General FAQ

## Duplicate Transaction

A duplicate transaction occurs when a customer believes the same payment has been charged more than once.

The customer should compare the transaction IDs, transaction amounts, dates, and merchant details.

If two separate successful transactions exist for the same intended payment, the customer should contact support with the relevant transaction information.

## Payment Successful but Merchant Did Not Receive the Money

If the payment shows as successful but the merchant claims that the payment was not received, the customer should verify the transaction status and transaction ID.

The customer should provide the transaction ID to the merchant and contact support if the issue remains unresolved.

The customer should avoid making another payment immediately until the status of the original transaction is confirmed.

## Information Required for Payment Disputes

The customer should keep:

- Transaction ID
- Amount
- Date and time
- Merchant details
- Payment method

## Security Rule

Never share or request sensitive authentication information through normal support conversations.

Never ask customers for:

- OTP
- UPI PIN
- Password
- CVV
- Full card PIN
