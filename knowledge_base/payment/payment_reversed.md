# Payment Reversed Policy

## What is a Reversed Payment?

A reversed payment occurs when a previously initiated transaction is returned or cancelled and the amount is sent back to the original payment source.

## Why Can a Payment Be Reversed?

A payment may be reversed because:

- The transaction could not be completed successfully.
- The bank or payment network rejected the transaction.
- The merchant did not complete the transaction.
- A transaction remained unresolved and was subsequently reversed.

## What Should the Customer Do?

The customer should check the transaction status and account statement.

If the amount has been reversed successfully, no further action may be required.

If the customer has not received the reversed amount within the applicable processing period, they should contact support with the transaction ID.

## Required Information

The customer should provide:

- Transaction ID
- Transaction amount
- Transaction date
- Payment method
- Relevant bank or account details requested through the official support process

## Security Rule

Never request:

- OTP
- UPI PIN
- Password
- CVV
- Full card PIN
