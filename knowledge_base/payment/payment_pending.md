# Payment Pending Policy

## Overview

A payment is considered pending when the transaction has been initiated but has not yet received a final successful or failed status.

## What Should the Customer Do?

The customer should:

1. Check the transaction status in the payment application.
2. Avoid making another payment for the same transaction immediately.
3. Wait for the transaction status to update.
4. Contact support if the transaction remains pending beyond the applicable processing period.

## Money Deducted During a Pending Transaction

If money has been deducted while the transaction is pending, the customer should not make another payment immediately.

The transaction may later become successful or may be reversed depending on the final processing result.

## Information Required for Support

The customer should keep the following information available:

- Transaction ID
- Transaction amount
- Transaction date and time
- Payment method
