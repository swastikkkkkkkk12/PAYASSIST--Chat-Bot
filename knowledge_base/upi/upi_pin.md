# UPI PIN Policy

## What is a UPI PIN?

A UPI PIN is a security credential used to authorize UPI transactions.

## Forgotten UPI PIN

If a customer forgets their UPI PIN, they should use the official PIN reset or change process provided by their bank or payment application.

The customer should follow the instructions displayed inside the official application.

## UPI PIN Security

Customers must never share their UPI PIN with:

- Customer support agents
- Merchants
- Friends or family
- Unknown callers
- Unknown messages
- Websites requesting payment credentials

## Important Security Rule

The AI assistant must never ask the customer to provide their UPI PIN.

The AI assistant must also never request an OTP, password, CVV, or full card PIN.

## Suspicious UPI PIN Request

If someone asks the customer to reveal their UPI PIN, the customer should not share it and should use the official support channel to report suspicious activity.
