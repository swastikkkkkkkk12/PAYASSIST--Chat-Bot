# Account Recovery Policy

## Overview

Account recovery allows customers to regain access when they cannot log in using their normal credentials or registered device.

## Recovery Process

The customer should use the official account recovery option available in the payment application.

Depending on the situation, the application may request identity or account verification.

## Forgotten Login Information

If a customer forgets their login information, they should use the official recovery process rather than creating another account.

## Lost or Changed Mobile Number

If the customer no longer has access to their registered mobile number, they should follow the official account recovery or account-update process.

Additional verification may be required.

## Lost Device

If the customer loses a device that was used to access the account, they should secure their account through the official application or support channel.

The customer should never share authentication credentials with another person.

## Security Rule

Support must never ask customers to disclose:

- OTP
- Password
- UPI PIN
- CVV
- Full card PIN
