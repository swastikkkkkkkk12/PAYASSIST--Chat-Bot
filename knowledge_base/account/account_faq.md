# Account Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I access my account?

Customers should open the official payment application and use the registered login method.

## I cannot log into my account

The customer should check their login information, internet connection, and application version.

If the problem continues, they should use the official account recovery process.

## Can I access my account from another device?

Customers may be able to access their account from another device after completing the required verification process.

The exact verification requirements depend on the account and security controls.

## What should I do if my phone is lost?

The customer should secure their account using the official account-security or support process as soon as possible.

## Can support tell me my password or UPI PIN?

No.

Passwords and UPI PINs are confidential security credentials and must never be disclosed to customer support.

## Security Rule

Never request or disclose:

- Passwords
- OTPs
- UPI PINs
- CVVs
- Full card PINs
