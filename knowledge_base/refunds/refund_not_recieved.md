# Refund Not Received Policy

## Overview

A customer may report that they have not received an expected refund even though the refund was initiated or marked as completed.

## Initial Checks

Support should verify:

- Original transaction ID
- Refund status
- Refund amount
- Refund initiation date
- Merchant information
- Refund ID, if available

## If the Refund Was Initiated

If the refund has been initiated, the customer should wait for the applicable processing period.

The refund is normally returned through the payment method or original payment source according to the applicable refund process.

## If the Refund Shows as Completed

If the refund shows as completed but the customer cannot locate the amount, the customer should:

1. Check the original payment account.
2. Review the account statement.
3. Verify the refund amount and date.
4. Contact support with the refund and transaction details if the amount is still missing.

## Important

Do not ask customers to provide:

- OTP
- UPI PIN
- Password
- CVV
- Full card PIN
